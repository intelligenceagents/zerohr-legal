ZeroHR Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · ZeroHR beta · Provided by intelligenceagents.ai · Contact yixiao@intelligenceagents.ai

ZeroHR is in beta. Payroll submission, benefit coverage, signatures and identity provisioning are simulated. Use synthetic or approved test information until your company has confirmed its own requirements.

Who we are

ZeroHR provides HR records, permissions and reviewed execution for companies, used through AI assistants such as ChatGPT. ZeroHR does not run its own AI model and does not train models on your data.

Why we process it

To provide the HR service your company configures, enforce permissions and independent approvals, keep audit evidence, deliver invitations you request, and secure and operate the service.

Who receives it

Supabase (authentication, database and storage, US West region); the connected systems your company chooses, such as Merge, Finch and the providers behind them; Stripe for billing when enabled; Resend for invitation email; and your AI assistant provider for information you request through it, under that provider's own terms. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Tax and pay records are kept four years and other documents seven years by default. Parsed upload summaries expire after 24 hours and unfinished uploads are removed after two hours. Audit history is kept for the life of the company account. A company owner can download all company data from the ZeroHR account page at any time, and can ask us to delete it; we confirm deletion within 30 days of a verified request. Deleted data then ages out of database backups on their normal schedule, and our email provider keeps delivery records under its own retention.

Your choices

Disconnect an assistant from your ZeroHR account page to revoke its access. A company owner can disconnect a connected system, which deletes its stored credential. Email digests are sent only if an owner opts in. To access, correct or delete your information, email yixiao@intelligenceagents.ai.

Security

Row-level security limits each company's data to its members. Approvals are recorded and checked against the approver. Provider tokens are encrypted. Download links expire after 60 seconds, and identity-form links are single-use and expire after 10 minutes.

Children

ZeroHR is a workplace tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new effective date. We will email company owners at least 30 days before a material change takes effect, unless it is required sooner for security or by law.
