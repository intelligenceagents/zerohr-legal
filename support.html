Getting started

In ChatGPT, open ZeroHR and ask "What needs my attention today?" or "Help me set up our company." Sign in at zerohr.live with your verified email.

Common questions

Where do I upload a W-4? Open your documents in ZeroHR and choose "Upload securely on zerohr.live". W-4, state withholding and I-9 forms never go through the chat.

Why does a connection say "Not configured"? That provider has not been enabled for this deployment yet. You can still send data from another connected app, a spreadsheet or a webhook.

Who approves changes? A different owner or HR reviewer. In a company with a single owner, that owner approves in the review card after confirming the exact change.

Is payroll real? Not yet. Payroll submission, benefit coverage, signatures and identity provisioning are simulated during the beta.
